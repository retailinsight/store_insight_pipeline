# Markdown On-Time Completion Compliance — Investigation Skill

## What the alert means
A store completed fewer markdowns on time than expected this week, either versus its own prior-week performance (WOW) or versus the chain average (VS_CHAIN), or both.

## Facts to review
- `daily_performance_last_7d`: day-by-day on-time completion rate and task volume.
- `worst_departments_top3`: departments with the lowest on-time completion rate.
- `driver_assessment`: whether the drop looks workload-driven (task volume rising) or coaching-driven (volume flat/falling but execution worse).
- `workload`: this-week and prior-week markdown volumes, chain average volume per store, and classification.

## Recommended response
- **WORKLOAD** (volume INCREASING): review staffing hours against markdown workload this week; escalate to area manager if hours cannot cover volume.
- **COACHING** (volume STABLE/DECREASING): review the markdown routine with the team within 48 hours; focus on the worst departments.
- Success target: recover to the prior week's on-time completion rate.
- Review in 7 days.
